## Design Context

### Users
Hiring managers, recruiters, and design directors at enterprise companies (AT&T-scale). They scan resumes in 10 seconds, then dig if interested. Desktop-first viewing. Print/PDF as fallback.

### Brand Personality
Confident, precise, unflinching. A 30-year UX veteran who also ships TypeScript AI tools. Not corporate polish. Not startup energy. The quiet authority of someone who has done the work.

### Aesthetic Direction
Extreme minimalism. Black and white only. Gray floor at rgba(0,0,0,0.55) - never lighter. Cabinet Grotesk (display, 900), Satoshi (body), Azeret Mono (labels/data) via Fontshare. Single thin line separator. Click-to-drawer interaction for depth. Letter-size page metaphor. Print-capable.

### Design Principles
1. **Narrative over inventory** - The page tells a story (who -> why care -> proof -> background), not a parts list.
2. **Earned depth** - Surface is sparse. Detail lives in drawers. Nothing competes for attention.
3. **Typography is the design** - No icons, no color, no decoration. Type weight, size, and spacing do all the work.
4. **Every pixel is a decision** - If it doesn't serve the hierarchy, remove it.
5. **Print-worthy** - Must look intentional printed on paper. No screen-only tricks.
